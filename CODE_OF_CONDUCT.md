# Team Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the expectations for communication, participation, and responsibility within our DevOps Scrum team. All team members are expected to follow these rules throughout the coursework.

### 1. Weekly Lab Work

* Labs should be completed within their designated week and before the following Monday.
* The Scrum Master will check that each team member has completed the required lab work.

### 2. Coursework Contributions

* Each team member must complete and submit their assigned work to the Scrum Master and Product Owner before the agreed deadline.
* Team members should communicate as early as possible if they are unable to meet a deadline.

### 3. Preparation

* The Scrum Master, Product Owner, and one rotating team member must review the relevant lecture and lab materials at least one week in advance.
* The additional team member responsible for preparation will rotate according to the agreed schedule.

### 4. Team Meetings

* All team members are expected to attend scheduled Discord meetings.
* If a member cannot attend, they should notify the team in advance where reasonably possible.

### 5. Respectful Communication

* All team members must communicate respectfully and professionally.
* Violence, threats, harassment, personal attacks, or heated arguments are not acceptable.
* Disagreements about the project should be discussed constructively and focused on the work rather than individuals.

### 6. No Personal Negativity

* Team members must not make personal or derogatory comments about other members.
* Concerns about another member's contribution should be raised constructively with the team rather than discussed negatively behind their back.

### 7. Shared Responsibility

* Every team member is responsible for contributing to the project and supporting the team's progress.
* Team members should communicate openly about difficulties, delays, or problems affecting their assigned work.

Any other notable offences that required addressing will be updated to this code of conduct as necessary, I will notify the members when any revisions happened.
